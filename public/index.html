Material Mapping maps a color to a material type. If no map is provided, a default diffuse surface is created.

Types supported:

  • Diffuse (Lambertian): Matte surface
  • Specular (Perfect Mirror) - color ignored
  • Glossy (Microfacet Reflection) - roughness 0=mirror, 1=diffuse
  • Transmission (Glass/Dielectric) - roughness 0=clear, 1=frosted
  • Emission: Emissive surface
